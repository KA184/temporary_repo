Regarding the offerId topic, it is an identifier that is created for each offer and is unique; therefore, OID1 and OID2 will have different values. Additionally, in this case, even if the products share the same capability, they are different products and therefore different offer requests (it could be the same capability but with different properties, such as a drilling capability with an M6 bit and another with an M7 bit). Since these are different requests, they not only have different offerId values, but also different proposalId values, as they are different offer request conversations.
